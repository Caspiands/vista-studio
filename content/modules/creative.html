Corporate identity applied

Every template uses the three logo colours plus white and one dark ink for text. The logo is never recoloured; on blue or dark backgrounds it sits on a white plate.

Palette and rules

Vista Blue#009FFFBackgrounds, CTAs, accents
Lime#A6E700Pillar and campaign tags, lime CTA, rails
Sky#62BBFFLens rings, image placeholders, sub-lines on dark
Ink#0B2740Headlines and body on light backgrounds
Tint#E6F5FFLight bands, soft tags, carousel bodies
  • Logo — vectorised from the supplied PNG as the Figma component Logo / Vista; clear space at least the height of the swoosh; minimum width 240 px on 1080 canvases, 120 px on display ads.
  • Type — Inter (Bold for headlines, Semi Bold for tags and CTAs, Regular for body) as a stand-in until Vista's brand typeface arrives with the brand files. Swap once, everywhere, through the Figma text styles.
  • Motif — concentric lens rings at 20–40% opacity, drawn from the concept site, never over the logo.
  • Compliance furniture — every ad carries a KKLIU line; every comparison slide carries the MAB 3/2023 line; image placeholders state the photography rule (Vista-approved, consented, no before/after).

Finished posts — November and December

Eight calendar posts composed on the templates with photography. The images are AI-generated concept photography in Malaysian settings so the posts read as real; before publication each is replaced with Vista's own consented photography or shot to the same brief (the asset checklist below). Every line of copy is written to sit in the MAB-exempt educational lane unless marked.

Finished ads — and why each one works in the DTR

One executed ad per campaign in the first quarter, each tied to the finding in the digital asset analysis it is built to fix. All six run in the KKLIU lane; the KKLIU line on each is a placeholder until the number is issued.

Organic post templates

Six templates cover the 8 counting posts a month (4 reels, 2 carousels, 2 static) plus the 16 Google Business Profile posts. Placeholders in square brackets are what changes per post.

Ad templates

Six templates cover Meta, Google Display, TikTok/Shorts and the landing-page pairing that keeps message match honest. All run in the KKLIU lane: briefed about six weeks earlier than organic posts.

Production workflow

At full pace three streams run at once. From brief to first post is about five weeks, which is why November is the ramp month.

Publish

This month's 8 posts, two a week (Tue/Fri), plus daily community management — replies within 4 business hours, price questions routed to WhatsApp with the approved answer.

Produce

Next month's 8 posts in design, video, internal QA and Vista review.

Prep campaigns

The next campaign's visuals, started 4–6 weeks before launch — about 11 weeks for creative that needs a KKLIU number.

The pipeline for every monthly batch

1 · Brief & plan3 working days

Slots filled against the weekly rhythm: Attract → Educate → Build trust → Convert.

2 · Design & video~8 working days

Templates above; BM re-cuts for TikTok; ZH version of the month's Mandarin post.

3 · Internal QA + compliance gate2 working days

Checked against MAB 3/2023 and the retired-claims list (F-22).

4 · Client review≤ 7 working days

Clinical sign-off included. Beyond 7 days the post moves to the next batch; approved evergreen fills the slot.

5 · Schedule & publishAcross the month

Scheduled posts publish on holidays; no production work on weekends or Selangor public holidays.

Weekly rhythm

  • Week 1 Attract — symptoms, habits, trends (K, S)
  • Week 2 Educate — compare options, bust myths (R, F)
  • Week 3 Build trust — clinics, team, technology (V)
  • Week 4 Convert — how to book, which branch, what's on (P)

The trending slot

  • Reel 3 (Screen Life) reacts to a live trend on a 24–48 h fast lane, from December.
  • Lifestyle and habits only — never treatment, never a practitioner — so it stays in the exempt lane.
  • Audio only from the platforms' commercial-use libraries; consumer trending tracks are licence-restricted for business accounts.
  • Needs a named Vista reviewer on same-day turnaround; otherwise the slot runs evergreen on the standard pipeline.

Compliance lanes

  • Lane 1 — exempt: educational posts, greetings, award announcements. CDS gate + Vista clinical review; 5-week pace.
  • Lane 2 — advertisements: anything naming a price, package, offer, campaign or event registration. KKLIU per creative; seasonal creative submitted six weeks before launch; approved evergreen runs if approval slips.
  • "Self-compliance for everything" is not offered to Vista.

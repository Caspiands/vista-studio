Budget note — Vista's Q1 figures, beside the planning assumption

Vista's Q1 2027 plan proposes RM180,000. Paid media RM54,000 (search RM30,000, social RM24,000), creative RM24,000, website and SEO RM12,000, offline activations RM36,000, partnerships RM9,000, collateral RM9,000, CRM and tracking RM9,000, reserve RM27,000 released at the CP2 mid-campaign review. Digital lines total RM99,000 for the quarter, about RM18,000 of media a month. The planning assumption in this roadmap is RM50,000 a month, and the 2026 run-rate was about RM75,000. Campaign weighting is set at the Day-30 media audit once Vista confirms which figure governs 2027. Both are shown until then. These are Vista's own plan figures. Marketing is accountable for qualified leads and booked consultations; Operations is accountable for attendance and conversion. Vista's quarterly checkpoints sit on the year as diamonds: CP0 17 Jan, CP1 31 Jan, CP2 21 Feb, CP3 14 Mar, CP4 at the end of March.

04Respond.io — where leads go

Takeaway: unpaid enquiries book at 14.4% and paid enquiries at 1.5%. These figures are conversations and contacts, not conversions. WhatsApp Business API starts a new conversation when a person replies more than 24 hours later, so one contact can be counted more than once. The lifecycle status (New, Warm, Hot, Booked) is a separate field and does not reset with that count. We reviewed 12 months of Respond.io reports — the maximum available on the platform — together with nine months of lifecycle data. This covers 95,031 contacts and 73,069 conversations, managed by four human agents and one AI agent, with the AI agent currently handling 57% of assignments.

23,616 contacts deleted in July and August 2026

This is a finding, not a conclusion. 14,018 contacts were deleted in July and 9,564 in August, 34% of the year’s intake, by one account. The rest of the twelve months does not show a deletion of this size, and once a contact is deleted there is no record of who they were. Deleting contacts also makes a conversion rate look better than the year actually was. Vista needs to say what happened. We have not treated it as a security incident, because the export does not show intent.

Phone calls are a gap

A call from Google Business Profile or the website call button does not land in Respond.io, so it cannot be tied to a campaign, a centre or a booking. The call counts exist inside Google Business Profile; what they became (a question, a walk-in, an appointment) does not. Call tracking is part of the plan. The tool is chosen with Vista. The point is that a call becomes a measurable enquiry, not that a product name is decided here.

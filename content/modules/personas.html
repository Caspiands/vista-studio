Target audiences first

Four audience groups, set by age and by who actually makes the decision. Every campaign and post targets one of these groups; the personas below are the people inside them.

Young adults 18–40

Decides for themselves
Wants to be glasses-free, or has been told LASIK is not suitable and needs the alternatives explained. Services: iLASIK, CLEAR PRO, ICL.
Personas: P01, P02, P10

Parents of children 5–17

The parent decides, the child is the patient
Worried that the child's myopia keeps rising. Services: kids eye examination, myopia control programme. Reached through parenting audiences, schools and optometrists — not through the child.
Persona: P03

Adults 40+

Decides for themselves, or arranges for a parent
The widest range of needs: presbyopia, cataract, glaucoma, dry eye. Includes the adult son or daughter arranging a parent's cataract surgery. Services: cataract, RLE, glaucoma, dry eye.
Personas: P04, P06, P07

Seniors 60+

Decides for themselves, often with family support
Wants to see the doctor, not type on a phone; comes on a neighbour's or family's recommendation. Services: cataract, glaucoma. Reached through Facebook, radio, Google Business Profile and the family member in the group above.
Persona: P05

P07 (dry eye), P09 (traveller from a city with no centre) and P10 (promo-driven shopper) are situations, not separate audiences — they are reached inside the age groups above with a different message. P08 (Singapore cross-border) is not a primary target: Singapore cannot be targeted as a whole, so it is held back as an option for the Johor Bahru centre only, if Vista wants it.

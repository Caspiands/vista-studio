A weekly structure for every month

Each week focuses on one stage of the patient journey. The team keeps output consistent and on-brand.

WEEK 1

Attract

Stop the scroll — symptoms, habits, trends. High reach.

WEEK 2

WEEK 3

Build Trust

Show the clinics, team and technology. Proof before the ask.

WEEK 4

Content pillars

Six content pillars, each with a content category, a clear job, a set of recurring series and the creative angles it takes. Every social post, campaign creative, landing page and article comes from the same pillars, so what someone sees in a post, an ad and on the website tells one story. Select a pillar to see its angles; the monthly slots it fills are highlighted on the left.

The monthly mix

Cross-published to Facebook and Instagram. Animation is a technique used inside these slots. The pillar mix is agreed with Vista each month — a spread across pillars, or one pillar as the month's theme.

    Campaign briefs

    One brief per campaign: who it is for, the evidence behind it, the message, channels, creative, landing page, how Respond.io handles the lead, and the KPIs it reports against.

    Monthly content plan — indicative topics

    Topic ideas for each month, in slot order, with the campaigns live that month. We call this our content. A piece written here may later be boosted, so the plan is not labelled organic or paid. These are the angles, not final posts: the month's posts are agreed with Vista at the monthly planning meeting. A trending slot for Gen Z can be turned around within 48 hours, because a trend is gone after that. Every piece still clears MAB 3/2023 or carries its KKLIU number.
